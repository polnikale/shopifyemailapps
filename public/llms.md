## Reading claims

Workflow exercises and timing examples are proposed plans, not observed merchant results. Inclusion in a directory is not a supplier ranking or proof of current availability. Historical messaging profiles can describe retired products; distinguish those products from the supplier's other services. Article narratives may differ from the catalog and must not be treated as verified by a page date. Use the supplier's current documentation and contract for plan, channel, integration and pricing decisions.
